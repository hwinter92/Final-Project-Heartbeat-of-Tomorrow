About the Designer

Hi, my name is Hannah Winter. I am in my third year at UW-Milwaukee and I’m majoring in Design and Visual Communications. The areas of focus I am interested in are logo design, poster design, and digital illustrations. I also have an interest in the practices of printmaking, photography, and the most recent is fibers. I love designing for topics I am passionate about, like this website. I am really into raising awareness for endangered animals, and sort of always have. I believe we all have the power to make a change by using our voice to speak on topics we are truly passionate about.

Why was this site created? The importance?

This site was created to be a interactive website version of a previous Intro to Design project I did. The project was to create a Social Cause Visual System for a current social issue. I chose to create digital illustrations or icons to raise awareness for endangered animals. I believe this project is important because endangered animals/species is an extremely relevant topic that is very urgent. This topic isn’t meant to be sugarcoated nor brushed under the rug. People don’t realize that animals are so important to how our world exists and how the circle of life is carried out. Especially in today’s world, the major threats are deforestation, global warming, climate change, industrialization, AI, waste, and oil spills.

What is the project? What audience?

The project was focusing on raising awareness on three different animals from three different ecosystems. This included the Northern White Rhino from the savanna, the North Atlantic Right Whale from the ocean, and the Polar Bear from the arctic. My designs and research was based off how the animals are doing now, and what their main threats that are causing them to be endangered are. The audience is directed towards anyone who supports conservation or protecting endangered animals, as well as people trying to make a change. It could also be directed towards people interested in learning more about these efforts, or learning the importance of how serious endangerment is.
