Digital Designs

The Northern White Rhino design was inspired by poaching being the main reason these rhinos are going endangered. I wanted to have a rhino head with emphasis on the horn because that’s the poachers prized possession. I also wanted to incorporate a poacher walking away with the horn to symbolize the illegal trade and how wrong it is. The color choice for the background was to mimic the gray of the rhino. I wanted the poacher’s horn to be white with a drop of red blood to really emphasize the threat. The words, “It shouldn’t have to be this way.” was to really hone in on the fact that endangerment is a serious topic and it honestly shouldn’t have had to come to this in general.

For North Atlantic Right Whale, I wanted to create a design that would be the whale swimming and doing well, thriving. I also wanted to create a design that would be the completely opposite, a skeleton of the whale. This was to highlight the threats to the whales being violently struck by vessels and getting caught in fishing nets. I chose a blue/teal color to incorporate the water since the whales are black. Once again, I emphasized the threat by making the boat a bright red color to stand out. I added the words, “They don’t just keep ecosystems alive, they keep us alive.” to show that these animals help our Earth stay balanced. They are key factors in the world and we should protect them at all costs.

Research

The Polar Bear design, I knew climate change was a huge impact for polar bears so I wanted to send that message of how they were thriving with pure ice and perfect spots for catching seals. To contrast this, I made the polar bear, skinny and starving, with no seal because hunting gets tough when there aren’t a lot of ice caps so hunt on, hence there only being one. Along with this I wanted to create waves around the polar bear to show how it’s all turning to water and the white framing of the polar bear is to show the isolation of not many polar bears being left in the wild. I also showed this through the two polar bears on the top and only the one on the bottom. I wanted to choose white to show the polar bear colors as well as the Arctic. I highlighted the threat of food scarcity due to the ice caps melting. The words, “Their time is running out. BE THE CHANGE.” felt extremely impactful to me, as did the other phrases. It felt like I could get a message out to everyone how urgent this issue is.
