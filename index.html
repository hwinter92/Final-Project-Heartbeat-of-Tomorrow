Welcome to Heartbeat of Tomorrow

Heartbeat of Tomorrow is a website designed for raising awareness about endangered animals. Specifically, the Northern White Rhino, the North Atlantic Right Whale, and the Polar Bear. This website is based off a project I did for addressing a social cause I was passionate about and creating three designs to support this cause. This website was carefully researched and put together to ensure all information is factual and up to date. My designs were created to be urgent and to make sure this issue is addressed. Endangered animals and their loss of habitats is a serious ongoing issue. Without change, it only gets worse, we are their only hope of protection. We must do our part to protect these animals as we co-exist together on Earth.
